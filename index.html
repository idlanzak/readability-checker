Document Readability Checker

Upload a document or paste text to calculate Flesch Reading Ease and the Gunning Fog Index. Your document is processed in this browser and isn’t uploaded to a server.

Choose a document
Supported formats: .txt, .pdf, .docx

PDF and Word files are read locally in your browser. Scanned PDFs need OCR and aren’t supported by this version.

For section-by-section scores, put each section heading on its own line and separate sections with ---. Example: Introduction, its text, ---, Methods, its text. Each section needs at least 20 words. Without separators, the whole document is scored as one section.

Results

These formulas are estimates for English prose. They use sentence, word, and syllable counts; results can be misleading for lists, dialogue, technical writing, or very short texts.
